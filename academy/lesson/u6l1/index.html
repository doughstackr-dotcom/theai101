Stops are structure

Where the idea is wrong — not where fear lives.

The only honest place

A stop belongs where your read is proven wrong: below the swing low that created a long setup, above the high that created a short. Anywhere else is a pain tolerance, not a plan.

Tight vs wide

A stop inside the noise gets hit by traffic even when the idea was right. A stop a mile away makes every loss enormous. Structure decides the stop; position size adapts to the stop.

Quick check

You go long on a hammer at support. The structural stop goes…
  • Just below the hammer wick / support low
  • A fixed 2% away, ignoring the chart
  • Above the hammer high
  • Wherever feels brave

If that low breaks, the rejection story is false. That is the definition of "idea proven wrong."

A stop that is too tight (inside the noise) mainly causes…
  • Getting knocked out by random wiggle
  • Bigger wins
  • Better sleep
  • Free entries

Noise will visit your stop before the idea gets its chance. Room to breathe, sized down to afford it.
