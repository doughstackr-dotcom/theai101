The pre-call script

A 10-second checklist before every prediction.

The script

1. Trend: up, down, or range? 2. Level: what is the nearest floor/ceiling? 3. Candle: who won the last complete one? 4. Call: does the next candle follow the script or fight it? 5. Write the call before the reveal.

Why writing it matters

A call you said out loud can be graded. A vague feeling cannot. The arena tracks your accuracy and streak precisely so your eye gets honest feedback instead of memories that flatter you.

Quick check

The most important habit the script builds is…
  • Deciding before the outcome is known
  • Trading bigger
  • Predicting every candle
  • Ignoring trends

Committing to a call first is what makes the review honest. Feedback after commitment is how eyes get trained.
