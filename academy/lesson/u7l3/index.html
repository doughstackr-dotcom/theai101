Unit 7 · The Live Lab

Keep the streak honest

Small samples lie. Routines do not.

Judge batches, not moments

Ten predictions mean almost nothing. Fifty to a hundred rounds start to mean something. Log a session, note accuracy, and look for drift: are you better on trending days? On higher timeframes?

What practice can and cannot do

Practice builds pattern recognition, discipline, and calm. It cannot make markets predictable — nobody can. The skill being trained is reading structure and managing decisions, and it transfers to every market you ever touch.

Quick check

After 15 arena rounds you are at 80%. The honest conclusion is…
  • A promising start — keep logging; 15 is a small sample
  • You have mastered the market
  • Predictions are guaranteed now
  • Time to trade rent money

Small samples flatter everyone. The number that matters is the one across hundreds of logged rounds.
