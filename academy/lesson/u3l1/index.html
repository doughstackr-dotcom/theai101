Engulfing — one side swallows the other

The most reliable two-candle reversal story.

The bullish story

A small red candle, then a green candle whose body swallows it whole. Sellers pressed, buyers answered with everything. That is a momentum flip in one bar.

The bearish mirror

Same script inverted after a rally: a big red body swallows the small green one. Late buyers are suddenly underwater.

Quality control

The engulfing body must clearly dwarf the swallowed one, and the pattern needs a real trend behind it. A "kind of big" candle in sideways chop is not an engulfing — it is a coin flip.

Quick check

A bullish engulfing works best when it appears…
  • After a clear decline, ideally at support
  • In the middle of a tight range
  • Only on 1-minute charts
  • After three green candles

Trend + location: the story is "sellers had control, buyers took it back." No prior decline means no story.
