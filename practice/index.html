REAL-DATA DRILL

Pattern Hunt on live charts

We scan real exchange candles with candle-math detectors. When a clean pattern completes, you get the chart — with the answer verified, not invented.

How the answers stay honest

Schematic quizzes teach the shape. Real charts teach the mess. This drill only scores patterns that a detector confirms in actual exchange klines — engulfing bodies checked against real bodies, hammers against real wicks. If the tape is quiet, it will tell you: mostly, real markets are noise.

REGIME DRILL

Trend or range?

The number-one beginner mistake is fighting the regime. Thirty real candles are on the table — call the regime before the reveal.

What you are training

Regime first, always. A perfect pattern against the trend fails more than a sloppy one with it. This drill grades your read against directional efficiency measured over the real window: how much of the path stuck versus backtracked.
