About

Why TheAI101 exists

Most trading education is either a wall of text, a $997 course, or a hype video. Almost none of it is built for how people actually learn charts: visually, by repetition, at their desk.

The idea

Charts are a language. You don't learn languages by reading grammar rules — you learn by seeing sentences until the patterns jump out at you. So every lesson here is drawn by code: candles animate into the patterns they form, trade overlays show you where the entry/stop/target go on the same chart, and the practice arena drills your eye until recognition is instant.

The merch

Then there's the gap between studying and trading: the moment you're mid-trade and need the reference. Shop-bought cheat sheets are... ugly. So we print our own — the exact patterns from the lessons, terminal-styled, on desk mats, mouse pads and posters. Learn it here, own it there.

The honesty policy

No "get rich" promises anywhere on this site. Most retail traders lose money — in stocks, options, forex, and especially binaries. What separates the survivors isn't a secret indicator; it's reading price honestly, sizing positions so no single trade matters, and surviving long enough for skill to compound. Every page teaches that — including the pages about the markets where that's hardest.

Disclaimer: TheAI101 is an educational site. Nothing here is financial advice, and no pattern or market offers guaranteed profits. Trade with money you can afford to lose.
